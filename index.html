    General Instructions

    1. Total duration of the examination is shown in the timer. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer at the top right will display remaining time.
    2. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using the colour legend below.
    3. Click on a question number in the palette to go to that question directly.
    4. To answer a question, select the option(s) / enter the value, then click Save & Next.
    5. To mark a question for review, click Mark for Review & Next.
    6. To change an answer, select the question, modify, and click Save & Next.
    7. To clear a response, use Clear Response.
    8. Marking scheme: +4 correct, −1 incorrect, 0 unattempted.
    9. Multiple-correct questions score only when the exact correct set is selected.
    10. Answers are hidden until final submission. Do not close the browser during the test — progress autosaves locally.
